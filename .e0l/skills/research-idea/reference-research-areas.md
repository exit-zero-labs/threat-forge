# Research areas

Reference for the `research-idea` skill. What each output file must actually establish, and what counts as evidence.

The standing rule across all of them: **a cited source or an admitted gap. Never a confident number with no origin.** Fabricated precision is the highest-severity tell in `anti-slop/copy.md`, and it is most dangerous here, where a laundered guess becomes a commissioning decision.

## product.md

- The problem, stated as something a specific person has, not as a category.
- How they solve it today — including "they don't, they live with it", which is a real answer and often the strongest signal.
- What makes the current solution bad enough to switch away from. Switching costs are the usual reason good products fail.
- Who does **not** have this problem. A boundary is worth more than a persona.

## market.md

- Who else serves this need, including adjacent and non-obvious substitutes.
- Where the gap is, stated concretely enough to be wrong.
- Whether the gap is unserved because it is hard, or because it is not worth serving. These look identical from outside and have opposite conclusions.
- Any evidence of demand that is not the founder's own intuition.

## business.md

- How this makes money, and from whom.
- What it costs to run at zero users, because near-zero idle cost is a standing product value.
- What it costs at the first scale that matters.
- The cheapest experiment that would falsify the revenue assumption.

## technical-feasibility.md

Lightweight. This is not a design.

- Does it fit the Class C stack, or does it need a deviation?
- Any hard blocker: a required integration that does not exist, a licence, a data source nobody sells, a platform rule.
- Anything that would push it off Cloudflare, since that is the standing default.

## references.md

Every source with its URL and what it was used for. A source listed but not used is padding; a claim without a source is the tell.

## summary.md

The recommendation, its confidence, and **what would change it**. That last part is the useful one — a recommendation with no falsifier is an opinion.

## next-steps.md

If commissioned: the first milestone's scope, what `M0 • POC` would have to prove, and the earliest point the idea could be killed cheaply.

## Handling thin evidence

Say the evidence is thin, in those words, and state what search you ran. An honest gap routes the decision to a human, which is the correct outcome. An invented figure routes it to a spreadsheet.
